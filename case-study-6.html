Sustainable Jewelry Brand: Boosting E-Commerce Sales with Social Media Advertising

Client: Amina's Handmade Jewelry | Service: Social Media Optimization & Paid Meta Ads

Background & Objectives

Facing fierce competition in the online boutique marketplace, Amina sought TIDA Ltd's expertise to elevate her sustainable jewelry brand, boost store traffic, and drive consistent daily sales.

  • Establish a strong social media presence to engage target consumers.
  • Drive over 500 daily website visits within 90 days.
  • Achieve a minimum 15% increase in e-commerce sales conversions.

Strategies Implemented

1. Profile Optimization & Content Calendar

Audited existing social profiles and developed an engaging content matrix blending behind-the-scenes craft stories with product showcases.

2. User-Generated Content (UGC) & Influencer Partnerships

Collaborated with niche fashion creators and incentivized customers to share unboxing videos and photos using dedicated brand hashtags.

3. Targeted Facebook & Instagram Retargeting Ads

Launched targeted conversion ads showcasing top-selling items and set up retargeting campaigns for visitors who viewed products without completing checkout.

Results & Impact

  • 200% Follower Growth across Instagram and Facebook within the first two months.
  • Surpassed 500+ Daily Visits to the e-commerce store.
  • 20% Sales Increase, exceeding initial benchmark expectations.
