Madam Azeezat Amologbe: From Inventory Backlog to Complete Sell-Out Success

Client: Madam Azeezat Amologbe | Service: Performance Ads & Direct Response Funnel

Background & Objectives

Madam Azeezat Amologbe needed an aggressive, high-converting paid traffic campaign to move significant inventory stock within a short time window.

  • Achieve complete inventory clearance within a 30-day campaign run.
  • Establish direct communication channels with high-value bulk buyers.
  • Maximize profit margins per sale through optimized ad spend.

Strategies Implemented

1. Direct-Response Offer Structuring

Positioned product bundles with compelling urgency triggers and clear, direct calls-to-action tailored to high-intent buyers.

2. Hyper-Targeted Localized Campaigning

Deployed geo-targeted Meta ad campaigns focused on commercial hubs across key business states in Nigeria.

3. Rapid Inquiry Handling Architecture

Connected ad campaigns directly to high-touch WhatsApp closing channels to process bulk orders instantly.

Results & Impact

  • 100% Sell-Out: Entire product inventory completely sold out ahead of the scheduled campaign deadline.
  • High Client Satisfaction: Established a lasting, high-trust relationship leading to multiple high-profile business referrals.
  • Record ROAS: Generated peak revenue return while maintaining exceptionally low ad acquisition costs.
