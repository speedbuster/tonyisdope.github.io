Aahit Optimum: White-Label Performance Marketing for a Top Canadian Agency

Client: Aahit Optimum (Canada) | Service: Media Buying & B2B Funnel Strategy

Background & Objectives

Aahit Optimum, a prominent Canadian digital agency, partnered with TIDA Ltd to manage high-stakes performance advertising and acquisition funnels for their North American client accounts. Facing rising ad costs in the US and Canadian markets, they needed an expert performance marketing team to optimize client ad spend and drive profitable lead volume.

  • Lower Cost-Per-Acquisition (CPA) across North American client campaigns.
  • Design bulletproof B2B and consumer lead qualification funnels.
  • Scale ad accounts without triggering platform penalties or ad fatigue.

Strategies Implemented

1. Hyper-Targeted North American Campaign Architecture

Restructured ad accounts using precise demographic, behavioral, and custom lookalike audiences targeting decision-makers in key Canadian and US metropolitan hubs.

2. Dynamic Copy & High-Intent Conversion Creative

Crafted direct-response visual assets and landing page messaging focused on business pain points, value propositions, and clear, low-friction conversion paths.

3. Advanced Retargeting & Lead Nurturing Pipelines

Built multi-stage retargeting campaigns combined with automated lead follow-up sequences to capture high-value prospects who left before converting.

Results & Impact

  • 32% Reduction in Cost-Per-Lead (CPL) within the first 30 days of media buying management.
  • 2.5x Increase in Qualified Lead Inquiries for high-ticket service packages.
  • Seamless Agency Partnership: Established a long-term, high-performing strategic alliance between TIDA Ltd and Aahit Optimum.
