How Subtle NG Scaled High-Ticket Travel Booking Inquiries via Meta Ads Funnels

Client: Subtle NG | Service: Lead Generation & Meta Ads Management

Background & Objectives

Subtle NG is a premier Nigerian travel agency providing flight bookings, luxury vacation packages, and visa advisory services. Despite high-demand offerings, they struggled with high cost-per-lead and tire-kicker traffic that rarely converted into paid consultations.

  • Filter out unqualified leads and capture high-intent travelers.
  • Establish an automated direct-to-WhatsApp conversion funnel.
  • Reduce overall acquisition costs while increasing booked travel packages.

Strategies Implemented

1. Audience Segmentation & Offer Positioning

We structured distinct ad sets targeting affluent travel enthusiasts, business travelers, and prospective holidaymakers in Port Harcourt, Lagos, and Abuja.

2. High-Converting Video & Carousel Ads

Deployed immersive video creatives showcasing destination highlights, client testimonials, and transparent breakdown guides for holiday packages.

3. WhatsApp Funnel Automation

Routed ad clicks directly into a pre-filtered WhatsApp funnel with automated qualification scripts to ensure sales reps spent time only on ready-to-book clients.

Results & Impact

  • 280% Increase in qualified travel consultation inquiries within 45 days.
  • 42% Drop in cost-per-qualified-lead across Facebook and Instagram campaigns.
  • Predictable Pipeline: Generated consistent weekly bookings for international and local tour packages.
